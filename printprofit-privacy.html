Privacy Policy — Print Profit

Android application “Print Profit” · Last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: Print Profit is a calculator. Everything you type stays on your device. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics and no advertising.

1. What you enter

Spool prices, print times, failure rates and the other figures you type are used only to calculate costs and a suggested price. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device so the app remembers them next time, and they are never transmitted anywhere.

2. No network use for calculations

Every calculation runs locally. No figure, quote or result is uploaded, and there is no server involved at any point.

3. Sharing a quote

If you choose Share quote, the app builds a plain-text summary and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes is entirely your choice — email, messaging, notes. The app itself does not send it anywhere.

4. Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app stores only a local flag indicating that the unlock was purchased, verified against Google Play.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only by the Google Play Billing library to complete and restore a purchase.

No camera, no location, no contacts, no files, no microphone.

6. What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No account, login, email address or personal identifiers.
  • No access to your photos, files, contacts or location.

7. Children

This is a general-purpose business tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent using the developer contact details published on the Print Profit listing on Google Play.

Prices produced by Print Profit are estimates based on the figures you enter. They are not financial advice. Check your local material and electricity prices, and the fees your marketplace actually charges, before setting a price.
