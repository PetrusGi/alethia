Privacy Policy — Passport Photo

Android application “Passport Photo” · Last updated 18 November 2026

Short version: your photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. The only data stored is what you choose to save to your own gallery.

1. Photos

When you choose a photo, the app reads that single image, detects the face, crops and rescales it, and discards it when you close the app. No photo is ever transmitted off the device. There is no cloud processing, no server and no upload of any kind.

Saving an export writes a JPEG to your device gallery. That file is yours and stays on your device unless you choose to share it.

2. Face detection

Face detection runs on-device using Google ML Kit with the face model bundled inside the app. Images are analysed locally and are not sent to Google or to us. Because the model is bundled, the app performs no network request in order to process a photo.

3. What we store

  • Your selected photo size (country/spec).
  • Whether you have used your free export.
  • Whether the one-time unlock was purchased.

These are stored in the app's private storage on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

4. Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. The app stores only a local flag indicating that the unlock was purchased, verified against Google Play.

5. Permissions

  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — used only on Android 8 and 9 to save your exported photo to the gallery. On Android 10 and later this permission is not requested at all.
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only by the Google Play Billing library to complete and restore a purchase.

No camera permission, no location, no contacts, no access to files you did not explicitly pick.

6. What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
  • No account, login, email address or personal identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or camera access.

7. Children

This is a general-purpose document tool and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent using the developer contact details published on the Passport Photo listing on Google Play.

Passport Photo is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government, passport office or immigration authority. Photo requirements differ per country and document; always confirm them with the issuing authority before submitting.
